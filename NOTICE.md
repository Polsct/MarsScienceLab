## NASA

This mod is a fan recreation of NASA's Mars Science Laboratory. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA, JPL
or any of the companies that built the spacecraft. Parts of the Curiosity rover mesh are derived from the Curiosity
Rover model published by NASA 3D Resources (NASA/JPL-Caltech). The rocker-bogie suspension meshes are derived from
the Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover model published by NASA 3D Resources (NASA/JPL-Caltech).
