# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-17)

First release, for KSP 1.12.x.

- Mars Science Laboratory at true size: cruise stage, aeroshell (heat shield, backshell, parachute), Sky Crane
  descent stage and the Curiosity rover.
- Guided entry towards Bradbury Landing and automatic EDL: parachute, heat shield drop, backshell separation,
  powered descent, sky crane lowering and flyaway. Every step can also be flown by hand.
- Curiosity rover with drive, arm, mast, drill and ten experiments.
- Size follows the home world (1:1 with RealSolarSystem, 0.625x stock, 0.76x JNSQ, 0.83x KSRSS) or a fixed scale
  in `MSL_Scale.cfg`.
- Two Contract Configurator contracts in career.
- Optional support for RealismOverhaul, RP-1, RealFuels, RealAntennas, Kerbalism, FAR, RealChute, Waterfall,
  MechJeb2 and Firefly.
- Install check in the main menu for missing dependencies, a misplaced folder or old mod folders.
